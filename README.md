## 🇬🇧 English

A one-stop user-research assistant with four capabilities. First, questionnaire design that produces a field-ready survey from the research goal, including pilot-fill optimization, delivered as a Word or Feishu doc. Second, interview outlines whose question count is set dynamically, with probe rounds, directions and trigger conditions for depth interviews. Third, open-ended verbatim tagging, using a five-step workflow to build a tag system and label at scale. Fourth, quantitative survey analysis that takes only collected data, auto-detects question types, auto-cleans, and reports key findings, personas, detailed findings and verbatim evidence. Trigger words include questionnaire design, satisfaction research, NPS, interview outline, depth-interview outline, verbatim coding, VOC analysis and survey analysis. A capability router picks the right module and loads its detail files on demand.

### ✨ What it does

- Questionnaire design from the research goal, with pilot-fill optimization
- Interview guides with dynamic length and probe rounds, directions, triggers
- Verbatim tagging: a five-step workflow builds tags and labels in bulk
- Quant analysis: only raw responses needed, auto question typing and cleaning
- Reports key findings, personas, detailed findings and verbatim quotes
- Delivers Word or Feishu docs; a router picks the right module
